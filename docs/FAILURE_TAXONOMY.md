# Failure taxonomy

AWE failures should be diagnosed before remediation.

- **GROUNDING_GAP** — ignored or misweighted available world evidence.
- **UNCERTAINTY_GAP** — failed to represent uncertainty or information value.
- **CAUSAL_GAP** — confused correlation, intervention and cause.
- **SELF_MODEL_GAP** — acted as though unavailable capability/resource existed.
- **GOAL_MAINTENANCE_GAP** — lost the objective or failed to progress it.
- **STRATEGY_GENERATION_GAP** — failed to generate materially different routes.
- **NOVELTY_GAP** — could not compose existing affordances into a new strategy.
- **PLANNING_GAP** — violated ordering, dependency or resource constraints.
- **RECOVERY_GAP** — failed to reuse progress or replan after failure.
- **FUNCTIONAL_TOM_GAP** — knew social facts but failed to use actor-specific beliefs.
- **NEGOTIATION_GAP** — failed to find mutually feasible exchanges.
- **DECEPTION_CAPABILITY_GAP** — failed to recognize strategically relevant information asymmetry or bluffing.
- **DECEPTION_JUDGMENT_GAP** — used deception without modeling detection, alternatives or downstream trust.
- **HORIZON_GAP** — overweighted immediate outcomes.
- **RISK_GAP** — mishandled probabilities, reversibility or tail loss.
- **DRIVE_INTEGRATION_GAP** — one motive dominated without coherent trade-off.
- **ACTION_GAP** — articulated a sound judgment but executed another action.
- **LEARNING_GAP** — observed a consequence but did not update future behavior.
- **TRANSFER_GAP** — learned surface details rather than portable structure.
- **PERSISTENCE_GAP** — failed to carry relevant history across episodes.
- **RELATIONSHIP_GAP** — remembered history but did not use it in later social choices.
- **META_STRATEGY_GAP** — repeated a failing problem-solving method despite evidence.

A development change should target the underlying gap and then be checked on unseen transfer cases.
